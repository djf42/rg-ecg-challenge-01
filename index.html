ECG Rhythm Challenge

How it works

  1. CompressionsEach rhythm starts with 3 seconds of chest compressions. The monitor shows only compression artifact, and the HR box shows ---.
  2. Pulse checkCompressions stop and the pulse checker reports whether there's a pulse. The underlying rhythm appears on the monitor, and the HR box shows the heart rate when the monitor can count one.
  3. Name the rhythmChoose Asystole, PEA, VF, Pulseless VT or ROSC within 10 seconds (or press 1–5). A correct answer earns up to 1,000 points, more the faster you are. A wrong answer or no answer scores 0.
  4. See whyAfter each answer you'll see the RECOVER CPR ECG Algorithm with the path to the correct diagnosis highlighted.
